# The WaaS Model — Website as a Service Explained

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- WaaS bundles design, build, hosting, ongoing edits, and SEO maintenance into one monthly line item.
- Total spend over the site's life is comparable to traditional, but cash flow is smoothed. No five-figure invoice the month before peak season.
- The vendor stops being a one-time contractor and becomes the closest thing to an in-house web team a small business can afford.
- Design and content drift constantly. A subscription assumes that drift instead of pretending the launch is the finish line.
- The lump-sum-plus-hourly model rewards your developer when you ignore the site and punishes you when you ask for help.
