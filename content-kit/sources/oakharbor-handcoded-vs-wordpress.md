# Difference Between Hand-Coded Websites and WordPress Sites

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Static sites are HTML, CSS, JS only. No database, no on-demand assembly.
- Mobile-first code at the top of the stylesheet reduces browser work.
- Google indexes mobile-first, so mobile speed is the ranking number that matters.
- Security: fewer moving parts means fewer doors for an attacker.
- Websites are an investment, not an expense.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- Hand-coded sites can load up to 10x faster than page builders.
- Static sites often clear under 0.5 seconds.
- Visitors abandon sites that take more than 3 seconds.
- Subscription pricing (~$175/month) vs lump sum (~$2,500+) for typical builds.

## Analogies in the original
- Spaghetti noodles vs organized stacks of code.
- Two cabins: the IoT-connected cabin with a dozen entry points vs a simple deadbolt cabin.
- House painting: anyone can paint a wall, but a pro finishes it cleanly.
- Surgery: you do not pick the cheapest surgeon.
