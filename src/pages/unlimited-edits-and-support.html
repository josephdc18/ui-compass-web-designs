What "Unlimited" Means

Unlimited means unlimited. You can update or change any page as often as you want, at no extra cost. Every change is included in the $150 monthly fee.

When you ask for a change, we share our perspective. If something would hurt your performance or SEO, we will tell you before we touch it. The final call is yours, and we deliver what you ask for.

Most requests are things like new text, swapped images, a new section here or there, or refreshing a page that has been sitting for a while. The two things outside the scope of unlimited edits are adding entirely new pages and converting one page type into another (for example, replacing an "About" page with a "Services" page), since the latter has measurable SEO consequences.

One client wanted a booking system on her site but had not picked a provider yet. Once she chose one, we integrated it into the site at no extra cost. The result is a website that grows with her business, managed end-to-end.

Most of our clients choose the Monthly Subscription for the predictability and the service. The work gets done, the invoice stays flat, and the relationship is long. It is the same arrangement we would want for our own business.
