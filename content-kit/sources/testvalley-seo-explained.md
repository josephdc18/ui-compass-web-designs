# SEO Explained — How You Get Ranked on Google

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- SEO is not a one-time fix. It is sustained technical and content work.
- One H1 per page that states the page's job clearly.
- Keyword stuffing is a penalty, not a shortcut.
- Indexing basics: clean nav, internal links, robots.txt, sitemap submitted.
- Speed is treated as a ranking factor in plain language.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- SEO results take roughly 6 months to materialize.
- Google expects pages to load in under 2 seconds.
- PageSpeed scores should hit 90+ with LCP under 2 seconds.
