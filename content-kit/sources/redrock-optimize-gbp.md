# Optimize Your Google Business Profile

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Primary category drives the most ranking weight. Be specific ("Italian Restaurant" beats "Restaurant"). Use up to 9 secondary categories most owners leave on the table.
- Description: 750 characters. Most owners write 200 generic ones and stop.
- Q&A is owner-fillable. You can pre-populate 5-10 questions you know customers ask, then upvote your own answers for prominent display.
- Google Posts expire after 6 months. Posting weekly is the minimum for the algorithm to read you as active.
- Photo cadence: at least one new photo per week. Logo, exterior, interior, team, work product. No stock, no flyers, no heavy filters.
- Top-three Map Pack listings average 40+ reviews. Velocity (2-3 per week) signals more naturally than 50 in one month.
- Complete profiles are 70% more likely to convert into location visits.
- NAP edits to name/address can take a week (manual Google review). Photos appear within a day.
