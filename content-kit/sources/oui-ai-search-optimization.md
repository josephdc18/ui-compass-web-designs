# AI Search Optimization (GEO)

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is its own discipline. Ranking in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews follows different rules than blue-link SEO.
- 90%+ of pages cited in ChatGPT do not appear in Google's top 20. The retrieval pool is broader than the ranking pool.
- 87% of ChatGPT citations match Bing's top 10. Getting indexed in Bing is a precondition for ChatGPT visibility.
- Most AI crawlers cannot reliably execute JavaScript. Page-builder sites that ship as JS-rendered shells are invisible to the model.
- robots.txt must allow OAI-SearchBot and PerplexityBot. Default builder configs often block them.
- Semantic chunking: each section must stand alone as a citable answer. 200-300 word self-contained blocks under question-shaped headers.
- Listicles, comparisons, and tables are 32.9% of all AI citations. Format matters as much as content.
- Co-citations: being mentioned alongside trusted brands signals relevance. Reddit and YouTube mentions feed the model.
- Schema types that matter for GEO: Article, FAQPage, Organization, ProfilePage, VideoObject.
- AI-referred traffic converts 3-5x higher than traditional organic.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- 90%+ of ChatGPT-cited pages don't rank in Google's top 20 (Ahrefs).
- 87% of ChatGPT citations match Bing's top 10 organic results (Seer Interactive).
- 11% of domains appear in both ChatGPT and Perplexity results.
- 32.9% of AI citations are listicles and comparisons (Semrush).
- 77% of ChatGPT users employ it like a search engine (Semrush).
- 3-5x higher conversion rates from AI-referred traffic vs traditional organic.
- 200-300 word ideal length for self-contained citable sections.
