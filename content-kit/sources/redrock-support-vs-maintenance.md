# Website Support vs Maintenance

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Maintenance is proactive and scheduled. Support is reactive and triggered.
- Maintenance: software updates, security patches, malware scans, backups, performance checks, uptime monitoring, scheduled content posts.
- Support: bug fixes, feature additions, integrations, troubleshooting, hack response, owner training.
- Cost split: maintenance $50-300/mo, support retainer $100-500/mo (typically 2-5 hours), per-incident support $100-300/issue.
- The interview question that exposes the gap: "if my site goes down at midnight, what happens?" If the answer is "open a ticket Monday," support is not included.
- Boundary example: posting next month's blog on schedule = maintenance. Rewriting your homepage because your services changed = support.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- Maintenance-only plans: $50-300/mo.
- Support retainers: $100-500/mo (2-5 hrs).
- Per-incident: $100-300/issue.
