# How to Respond to Negative Reviews

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- The audience for your reply is not the angry reviewer. It is the next prospect reading the thread to size you up.
- Six-step framework: use the name, thank them, validate the experience without admitting fault, give one sentence of context, take it offline, keep it under five sentences.
- A 4.2-star rating with thoughtful replies beats a 4.8 with silence. Perfection looks fake.
- Mistakes to avoid: replying angry, naming staff, blaming the customer, copy-paste templates, offering refunds for review removal (against platform terms).
- Customers expect a response within a week. Past that, the silence becomes its own review.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- 88% of consumers are more likely to use a business that responds to all of its reviews (verify source).
- Nearly half of consumers will skip a business that ignores reviews (verify).
- A 4.2-star average with thoughtful replies outperforms a 4.8 with ignored complaints.
- Recommended response window: under one week.
