Getting Your Website on the Internet

Once your website has been designed and developed, the next priority is getting it online so that customers can find it and engage with your business. This is where hosting and domain management come in — and they are areas where many businesses underinvest.

A high-performing website cannot run on inadequate infrastructure. Your site can be beautifully designed, well-coded, and professionally written, but if it is hosted on the wrong platform, the entire investment is undermined.

Why Hosting Deserves Serious Consideration

Your website is a collection of files stored on a server that is connected to the internet at all times. When a visitor enters your address, their browser requests those files from the server and renders them on screen.

If that server is slow, overloaded, or unavailable, your visitors will encounter error messages, long load times, or blank pages. The most common outcome is that they leave for a competitor's site.

If you have invested in working with an agency, you clearly value the quality of your website. Hosting is one of the most important decisions in that investment.

The Cost of Cheap Hosting

Budget hosting plans advertised at $9.99 per month look attractive, but the savings rarely outweigh the operational cost.

Low-cost providers typically place hundreds — sometimes thousands — of websites on a single server. The result is the digital equivalent of routing a highway's traffic onto a country lane: performance suffers across the board, page loads slow to a crawl, and visitors lose patience.

Beyond performance, budget hosting often comes with limited security, minimal customer support, and servers located far from your customers. The combined effect is a slower, less reliable website that undermines the work invested elsewhere.

Why We Choose Netlify

We host with Netlify because their infrastructure is built for the kind of fast, reliable websites we deliver.

Netlify operates a global Content Delivery Network (CDN). Rather than serving every request from a single location, your site is replicated across servers worldwide. When a visitor lands on your site, they are served from the server closest to them, ensuring rapid load times regardless of geography.

If you are based in Arlington, Texas, but a visitor accesses your site from Sydney, the visitor's experience will be just as fast as a local one. They are not waiting for data to travel across continents — it is delivered from a regional edge node.

Security is equally robust. Netlify is purpose-built for static websites, which have no databases and minimal attack surface, providing strong protection and continuous uptime.

Choosing the Right Domain

If you already own a domain, the process is straightforward. We can transfer it to our registrar, Porkbun, and manage hosting, DNS, nameservers, and SSL certificates on your behalf.

If you do not yet have a domain, we will help you select the right one. The cost of the domain is included in our monthly website package.

An effective domain is short, simple, and easy to remember. Where it is feasible, including a service or location keyword in the domain can also support discoverability.

How Should I Host My Website?

The most reliable approach is to work with a professional team. Self-hosting is technically possible, but it carries significant operational risk: a single power outage or connectivity issue can take your website offline for hours.

By hosting with Netlify, your website benefits from hundreds of servers across multiple regions, ensuring continuous availability and security regardless of any single point of failure.

We also maintain continuous monitoring across all client websites, with alerts in place to address any issue as soon as it arises.
