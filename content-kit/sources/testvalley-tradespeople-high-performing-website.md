# Why Tradespeople Need a High-Performing Website

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- A polished site lets a tradesperson charge more for the same work. Perceived value travels straight into the quote.
- Even a fully-booked, referral-fed business should still have a real site. It is the rate-raise lever, not just the lead engine.
- Photos of finished jobs do more selling than any paragraph. Trades is a visual sale.
- A site lets you pre-qualify the work you want (kitchens, panels, tankless) and politely route the work you do not.
- Booking-form friction (too many fields, no tap-to-call) drops trades leads disproportionately because the customer is usually on a phone outside.
