# Our Pay Monthly Website Pricing Explained

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- The traditional model (lump sum + hourly edits + new redesign every few years) hides total cost.
- A subscription bundles build, hosting, edits, and a periodic redesign.
- Sites visibly age every 3 years. Trends shift, plugins break, design language drifts.
- Monthly pricing removes the upfront barrier that scares small businesses.
- Per-edit billing punishes the client for keeping the site fresh.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- Three-year traditional total: ~£7,200 to £8,300 (£3,299 build + £1,404 hosting + £2,520 to £3,600 edits).
- Three-year subscription total: ~£6,444 (£179/month).
- Hourly edit rates: £70 to £100/hour.
- Design trends shift roughly every 3 years.
