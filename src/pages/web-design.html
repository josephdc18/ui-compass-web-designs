Website Design For Small Businesses

If your website looks dated, slow, or generic, visitors won't trust you. Your site is the first impression of your business, often before a phone call or an email is ever sent. If someone lands on it and thinks it looks five years old, can't find what they need, or struggles to read the text, they're gone. Probably to the competitor whose name you'd rather not say out loud.

You know the one I mean.

Every choice we make is in service of one goal: helping you stand out and turning visitors into enquiries, calls, or booked meetings. Your website needs to feel like you. Same voice, same priorities, same way you'd actually talk to a customer sitting across the table.

What Makes a Good Design Anyway?

A few things separate a website that converts from one that just exists. It is not only the look and feel. It is the content, the copy, and where both sit on the page.

Useful questions to ask yourself:

  • Does the design look crisp and current, or like it was built in 2018? Modern does not mean trendy. It means nothing feels out of place, and your identity still comes through.
  • Do you sound like you, or like a marketing brochure? Generic copy is easy to spot. So is AI-written filler: endless em-dashes, "in today's digital age," "truly transformative." You have read it a hundred times.
  • Is it obvious what you do? A 10-year-old should be able to land on the page and tell you what your business does. If they can't, neither can a busy buyer with three other tabs open.
  • Are your images yours, and are they sharp? Real photos of you and your team beat stock every time. But sharp stock beats a blurry, badly cropped photo of you.
  • Does the site have a clear path through it? Most service businesses need:
    • Homepage — where someone landing from search or word-of-mouth starts
    • About — because people buy from people
    • Services — what you do, what it costs, and how to find out more
    • Portfolio or Testimonials — proof you're as good as you say
    • Contact — easy to reach you, ask a question, or book a call

How Do We Design Our Websites?

Every client we talk to is different, and every website should feel that way. You came to us because you want professionals who can showcase what you do in your voice. Our job is to build something you're proud to show off.

The first step is understanding the people behind the business. We listen to what you offer, who you serve, and what makes the way you work actually different. From there, we get the facts and numbers of the business down on paper, and use that as the starting point for the design.

We design in Figma, the same tool most agencies and in-house design teams use. Its collaboration is solid, so we can share the file directly with you, iterate quickly, and make changes without long email chains.

A few things have to be right on your website:

  • Responsiveness: Most of your visitors are on a phone. Some are on an ultrawide monitor. Your site needs to work cleanly on all of it, and that starts at the design stage, not patched in afterward.
  • Accessibility: Color choices and layout have to work for everyone, including people who are colorblind or get overwhelmed by busy, cluttered pages. Cleanly broken-up content that is enjoyable to read is the baseline, not a bonus.
  • Clarity: Whoever lands on the site should know what you do and where to click next. No guessing.
  • Trust: The site has to build trust quickly, but in an authentic way. Not because it shouts, because it reads like a business that takes itself seriously.

We handle content and copy too. We can source stock images and write copy if you need it. If you would rather supply your own, we share a Google Drive folder where you drop in what you want on the site.

Once the design is approved, we move to development.

Free Redesigns Every 3 Years

We offer two pricing options: one-time or monthly. Most clients choose monthly. Honestly, most small businesses can't justify a $3,000–$10,000 upfront spend on a website, and they shouldn't have to.

Most websites start to look tired after about three years. Trends shift, technology moves on, and what felt fresh a few years ago reads like a relic. That's where the monthly plan earns its keep. The cost spreads across three years and bundles everything together: design, development, hosting, domain, unlimited edits, and all the small stuff that adds up over time. At the three-year mark, when the design starts feeling dated, we fully redesign and rebuild the site at no extra charge.

Your website stays fresh, current, and competitive for as long as your business is running, without the surprise invoices or "oh, you need an upgrade" conversations.
