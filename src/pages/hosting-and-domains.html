Getting Your Website on the Internet

OK, so your website is designed, developed, and beautiful. But right now it's just sitting on a computer as a few files of code, doing nothing. To make it useful — so customers can actually find it and you can start booking calls — it needs to be online. That's where hosting and a domain come in. And spoiler: most people get this part wrong.

You wouldn't put a brand-new engine in a car with four flat tires and a cracked frame. Same goes for your website. A great design, clean code, and professional copy don't matter if you cheap out on the layer that actually serves it.

Why Hosting Deserves Serious Consideration

Here's the part most people miss. Your website isn't floating around in cyberspace. It's a folder of files sitting on a computer (called a server) that's connected to the internet 24/7. When someone types your address, their browser asks that server for those files and renders them on screen.

If the server is slow, overloaded, or offline, your visitor gets nothing. Spinning loaders, error pages, or a blank screen. You know what they do next. They go to your competitor.

If you've decided to work with an agency, you obviously care about your website. Please don't cheap out on the layer that actually serves it.

The Cost of Cheap Hosting

You'll see ads for "website hosting at $4.99 a month!" and think you've found a great deal. You haven't.

Budget hosts stack hundreds, sometimes thousands, of sites onto a single server. When one of those sites spikes, every other site on that machine slows to a crawl. Page loads stretch out, visitors lose patience, and you have no idea why your traffic is suddenly bouncing.

It gets worse. Cheap hosting usually comes with weak security, almost no customer support, and a single server in a single location that's probably nowhere near your actual customers. The result is a site that feels sluggish for everyone and undermines all the work that went into building it properly.

Why We Choose Cloudflare Pages

We host on Cloudflare Pages because it never lets us down.

Cloudflare runs one of the largest content delivery networks (CDNs) on the planet — 330+ cities worldwide. Your site doesn't live on one server in one location. Copies of it are pushed out to every one of those edge locations the moment a build deploys. When someone visits, they get the version served from the location closest to them.

So if you're based in Arlington, Texas, and a customer pulls your site up from Sydney, they aren't waiting on data to travel across an ocean. They get it from an edge node in Sydney. Same site, same speed, anywhere in the world.

Security is built in too. Cloudflare Pages is purpose-built for static sites — no database to compromise, no admin panel to break into, no plugins to exploit. DDoS protection and SSL come standard, with no extra step on your end.

How Should I Host My Website?

The best way to host your website is to work with someone who does this professionally. You can technically self-host from your house, the same way you can technically cut your own hair with garden shears. It will be a haircut. It won't be a good one.

A single power outage or a flaky home internet connection can take your site offline for hours. That's why we use Cloudflare Pages, which runs across hundreds of edge locations worldwide. Even if one entire region goes down, your site stays up everywhere else.

We also keep continuous monitoring on every client site, with automatic alerts when anything looks off. By the time you'd think to check, we're already on it.
