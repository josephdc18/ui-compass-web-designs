# Google Reviews Removed (Without Warning)

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Google removes legitimate reviews silently. No warning, no notice, no explanation, no appeal that works.
- Businesses lose review counts overnight, sometimes 5-10 reviews at a time, with no way to recover them.
- Google has no obligation to explain. "They don't have to" is the operative phrase.
- The fix is platform-independence: capture testimonials on your own site, with permission, where you control the data.
- A testimonial on your own site is a permanent asset. A Google review is rented.
- Schema-marked testimonials on your site can show in search results AND survive any future Google review wipe.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- None hard-numbered. The "no warning, no notice" pattern is anecdotal but consistent.
