Freq. Q's

Why Not Hire a Full-Time Designer?

At Kem Design, we believe in affordability and flexibility. Wave goodbye to the burden of hiring a full-time designer, with costs exceeding $100,000 per year and no guarantee of a consistent workload. With Kem Design, you can pause your subscription during periods of lower design needs, ensuring that you only pay for design services when you genuinely require them. Our subscription model grants you the freedom to adapt and allocate resources wisely, without compromising on the expertise of our senior-level designers. Experience cost-effectiveness and convenience without sacrificing quality with Kem Design.

How Many Design Requests Can I Submit?

Once you become a subscriber, the design queue becomes your canvas. Add an unlimited number of design requests, and watch them come to life one by one, ensuring each project receives the attention it deserves.

When Will I Receive My Designs?

Most requests are completed within two days or less. For more intricate projects, additional time may be needed to ensure exceptional results.

How Do I Request Designs?

We prioritize your convenience and offer remarkable flexibility in requesting designs. Whether you prefer direct communication through Trello, sharing Google Docs or wireframes, or even recording a brief video instead of writing out your requirements, we've got you covered. As long as it can be linked to or shared within Trello, we're ready to bring your ideas to life.

What If I Don't Like the Design?

Not a problem! We will revise any design until it exceeds your expectations.

Are There Any Hidden Costs or Setup Fees?

Rest assured, our subscription fee is all-inclusive, with no hidden costs or setup fees.

What Happens at the End of My Subscription?

To ensure uninterrupted access to our services, your subscription will automatically renew at the end of each subscription period, unless you decide to cancel. You can have peace of mind knowing that your billing will be processed for the upcoming period, allowing our seamless design services to continue without any disruptions.
