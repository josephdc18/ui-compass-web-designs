# Why Custom-Coded Websites Are Best for Small Businesses

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Each major builder has its own failure mode: WordPress (plugin bloat), Wix (slow), Squarespace (template lock-in), GoDaddy (basic), Webflow (JS-heavy), Ionos (limited).
- WordPress databases are downloadable, which is a security framing.
- Builders become roadblocks once your business needs anything off the template.
- Hands-on agency model: client stays out of the dashboard.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- WordPress powers around half the internet.
- Wix sites can take 6 to 7 seconds to load.
- A site over 2 seconds loses around half the audience.
- 90+ Google PageSpeed scores from a hand-coded build are achievable.
