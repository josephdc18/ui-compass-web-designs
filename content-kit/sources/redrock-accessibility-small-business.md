# Website Accessibility for Small Businesses in 2026

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- ADA web lawsuits keep growing year over year and small businesses are the easy target because they settle instead of fighting.
- Roughly a quarter of US adults have a disability and that buying power is the largest under-served audience most owners ignore.
- Overlay widgets do not satisfy the law and the National Federation of the Blind is on record against them.
- WCAG 2.1 AA is the de-facto courtroom benchmark even though the ADA itself predates the web.
- Accessibility work overlaps with SEO because both reward semantic HTML, real headings, and descriptive alt text.
- The IRS Disabled Access Credit (Form 8826) covers half of eligible costs up to a $5,000 credit per year for businesses under 30 employees or under $1M revenue.
- The aging baby boomer cohort means accessibility is also future-proofing your customer base, not just compliance theater.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- Over 5,000 ADA web lawsuits filed in 2025.
- Settlements typically land between $5,000 and $25,000.
- 95.9% of the top one million sites fail basic WCAG checks (WebAIM Million, verify the year).
- 26% of US adults live with a disability (CDC, verify).
- $13 trillion in global disability spending power (commonly cited figure, verify source).
