A small business homepage is not an art project. It is a salesperson with seven jobs.

When the sections are wrong, in the wrong order, or missing — you don't lose conversions. You never had them.

Here's the order that actually works.

1. The Promise.
Headline that names what you do, in five words or less. "Plumbing for North Dallas." Not "Welcome to our website."

2. One CTA above the fold.
A single visible action — call, book, quote. Not three. The visitor's eye gives you one slot.

3. Trust signals before the brag.
Real photos, named testimonials, dated reviews. Before the "Why Choose Us" section. Otherwise you're a stranger telling them you're great.

4. The audience-mirror.
"For homeowners in Plano who want their kitchen done in one trip." Specific beats clever. Vague headlines get scrolled past.

5. The deliverable.
Show what they actually get. Photo of a finished job, a sample report, a 60-second video walkthrough. Otherwise it's abstract.

6. The objection-handler.
The thing they're worried about, addressed before they have to ask. Pricing. Timeline. Mess. "Same-day quote, no in-person sales pitch."

7. Tap-to-call, footer-locked.
60% of your traffic is on a phone. The CTA they don't have to scroll back up for is the one they use.

Skip one and you lose conversions you'll never measure.
