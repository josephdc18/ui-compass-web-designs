# How Custom Code Beats DIY Page-builders: The Clear Advantage of Hand-Coded Websites

In today's digital landscape, your website is often the first impression potential customers have of your small business. While platforms like WordPress promise easy DIY solutions, hand-coded websites offer superior performance that can make a real difference in your online success. Let's explore why custom code is the better choice for your small business website.

## Understanding Hand-Coded vs WordPress Websites

Think of it this way: WordPress sites are like prefabricated homes assembled from pre-made parts, while hand-coded websites are custom-built from the ground up. WordPress uses a content management system (CMS) that pulls content from databases and assembles pages on demand. This means every time someone visits your site, WordPress needs to gather all the pieces and put them together before showing the page. In contrast, hand-coded websites are built using pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript - the fundamental building blocks of the web. They're already complete and ready to serve instantly to your visitors.

## The Hidden Costs of WordPress

While WordPress might seem like an attractive option with its low initial cost and DIY appeal, it comes with significant drawbacks. First, there's the constant maintenance burden. As a small business owner, you'll need to regularly update the WordPress core software, themes, and plugins to maintain security and functionality. These updates can break your site's design or features, requiring additional time and potentially expensive fixes.

Security is another major concern. WordPress sites are popular targets for hackers due to their widespread use and numerous potential vulnerabilities through plugins and themes. Each plugin you add is another potential security risk, and keeping them all updated becomes a time-consuming task that falls on your shoulders. Do you really want to spend your valuable time managing website updates when you could be focusing on growing your business?

## The Clear Benefits of Hand-Coded Websites

Hand-coded websites offer several compelling advantages that make them the superior choice for serious businesses:

**Speed and Performance**: Hand-coded sites load dramatically faster than WordPress sites - often in under a second compared to several seconds for WordPress. This is crucial because studies show that most visitors will abandon a site that takes more than three seconds to load. Faster loading times mean better user experience and higher conversion rates.

**Security**: With no database or plugins to exploit, hand-coded websites are virtually impenetrable to common hacking attempts. They're like a solid brick wall compared to WordPress's house of cards - there are simply fewer points of vulnerability for attackers to target.

**Search Engine Optimization**: Google's algorithms favor fast-loading, mobile-friendly websites. Hand-coded sites are built with a mobile-first approach and clean, organized code that search engines can easily understand and index. This can lead to better search rankings and more organic traffic to your business.

**Customized User Experience**: By taking the time to do things the right way the first time, we can control every aspect of a users experience. Everything from the size of the buttons on a mobile device, to how long animations last (that sure can be annoying, right?) is on the table, and able to be adjusted.
