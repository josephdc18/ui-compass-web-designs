What "Unlimited" Means

Unlimited means unlimited. You can update or change any page as often as you want, at no extra cost. Everything is bundled into the $150/month.

When you ask for a change, we'll also weigh in on whether it's a good idea. If something would hurt your performance or SEO, we'll tell you before we touch it. But it's your website. If you want the change, you get the change.

Most requests are things like new text, swapped images, a new section here or there, or refreshing a page that's been sitting for a while. The two things outside the scope of unlimited edits are adding entirely new pages, and converting one page type into another (for example, turning your "About" into a "Services" page — that one's a SEO own-goal anyway).

One client wanted a booking system on her site but hadn't picked a provider yet. She decided to wait, picked one a few weeks later, and we integrated it into her site at no extra cost. She got a website that grows with her business, managed end-to-end. Win-win.
