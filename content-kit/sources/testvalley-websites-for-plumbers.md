# Websites for Plumbers — How a Better Website Generates More Leads

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- For trades, Google is the front door. Most plumber leads start with a "near me" search.
- People searching for a tradesperson stay inside a roughly 15-minute drive radius.
- Trust signals matter more for in-home services. Photos of the actual team move the needle.
- Location pages targeting nearby towns capture local search intent.
- Lead generation has a 6-month ramp before consistent inbound starts.
- A slow site fails before any of the trust work matters.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- 60% of users abandon sites that do not load within 3 seconds.
- Lead generation typically takes about 6 months to stabilize.
- Service-area sweet spot: nearby towns of 5,000 to 30,000 population.
- Roughly £3,000 baseline for a properly built custom site (UK market).
