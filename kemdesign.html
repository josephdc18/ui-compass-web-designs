What makes Kem Design different from other agencies?

We cut the fluff. No bloated processes, no inflated fees, and definitely no overcomplicated communication. Our streamlined approach focuses solely on what’s essential to get you the results you want. By eliminating unnecessary layers, we’re able to keep our subscription fees low while providing access to senior-level design talent that would typically come with a hefty price tag.

Why not just hire an in-house designer or freelancer?

Bringing on a senior in-house designer can easily cost six figures, not to mention the time (and headaches) it takes to recruit, onboard, and manage them. Freelancers, while cheaper, often come with their own set of challenges—unreliable timelines, inconsistent availability, and creative inconsistencies when you’re juggling multiple freelancers for different projects. At Kem Design, we give you access to a top-tier team, minus the hassle, minus the high cost, and with consistent results.

How fast will I get my designs?

It depends on the project, but most design requests are completed within 2-3 days. Larger or more complex projects may take a bit longer, but we break those down into milestones to keep things moving smoothly and on schedule.

How can I give feedback on my designs?

We’ve made feedback easy and flexible. You can share your thoughts directly in your project management dashboard, drop comments in Figma, or chat with us over Slack. Whatever works best for you!

Do you offer refunds?

We pour a lot of heart and soul into every design we create, and we stand by our work. Due to the amount of effort, attention, and quality that goes into our services, we do not offer refunds.

How do I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel my subscription?

Easy! Just shoot us an email at hello@kemdesign.co with your desired update, and we’ll handle the rest.

Upgrades: Costs are prorated during your current billing cycle.

Downgrades: Your plan will adjust to the lower level on your next billing date.

Cancellations: Your subscription will end at the close of your current billing cycle.
