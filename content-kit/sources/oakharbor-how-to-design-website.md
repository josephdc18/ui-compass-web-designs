# How to Design a Website

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Design follows a repeatable workflow, not formal training. Inspiration, paper sketch, Figma, build.
- Visual hierarchy and white space carry the layout more than colors do.
- Consistent spacing and type scales make a site feel intentional.
- A standard small business site has predictable sections: landing, services, about, content, gallery, testimonials, footer.
- Testimonials double as social proof and indexable keyword content.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- Section padding around 50px on mobile and 75 to 100px on desktop.
- Body line height of 1.4 to 1.5em.
- Headings at least 1.5x the body font size.
- Long-form content sections of around 1,200 words help SEO.
