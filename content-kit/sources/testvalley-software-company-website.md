# What Kind of Website Does My Software Company Need?

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- A single long landing page cannot rank for the spread of keywords a real business has. One URL means one shot at organic.
- Different visitor intents need different pages. Cramming product, pricing, case studies, and contact into one scroll confuses the buyer.
- One-page sites read as throwaway. They erode the credibility a small business is trying to build.
- Pricing on the site (even a starting band) qualifies leads before they reach your inbox. Hiding it costs you time, not deals.
- Case studies and testimonials with real names beat generic claims for both conversion and SEO.
- Page builders and heavy plugins still drag the load below the 2-second mark these sites need to hit.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- "Mobile devices account for 54% of website traffic." (corroborate against StatCounter; the number drifts year to year)
- Recommends sub-2-second load on every page.
