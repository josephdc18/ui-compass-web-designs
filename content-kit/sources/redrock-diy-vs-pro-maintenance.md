# DIY vs Professional Website Maintenance

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Five-question decision framework for owners: How big is the site? Does it generate revenue? How often does it change? How many plugins? What is your downtime tolerance?
- Two yes answers (revenue + frequent updates) and you should not be the one running maintenance.
- Honest time cost: 3 to 6 hours per month if done thoroughly, 4 to 8 for complex WordPress.
- At a $75/hr personal billable rate, 4 hours of monthly maintenance is $300 of opportunity cost. That already eats most pro plans.
- Underestimated risks: clicking "Update All" can cascade plugin conflicts and full outages, a wrong DB query wipes customer records, badly tuned cache plugins serve broken pages to half your visitors, and a single server flag can disable security.
- SEO regression is silent. Misconfigured robots.txt, redirect chains, or CWV drift erodes traffic without obvious symptoms.
