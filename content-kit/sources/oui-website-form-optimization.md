# Website Form Optimization

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- The 3-to-5 field rule: every field above 5 costs measurable conversion.
- Cutting fields from 4 to 3 lifts conversion roughly 50%. Cutting from 11 to 4 lifts it 160%.
- Making phone optional (not removing it) prevents 37% of abandonment.
- Multi-step forms beat single-page for any form with 5+ fields. Lift can hit 86%. One case went from 11% to 46% conversion.
- Inline validation (validate as user types, not on submit) cuts errors 22% and completion time 42%.
- The word "spam" near a form drops conversion 18.7%. "We won't spam you" reminds them spam exists.
- "Get my quote" beats "Get a quote" by ~25%. First-person CTA copy.
- Mobile keyboard types matter: type="email", type="tel", type="number". Wrong keyboard = abandoned form.
- Baseline form abandonment is 68-69%. 81% of users abandon at least one form per year.
- Each second of page delay (0-5s) drops form conversion ~4.4%.
