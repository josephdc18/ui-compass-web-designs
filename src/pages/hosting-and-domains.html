Getting Your Website on the Internet

Once your website is designed and developed, it needs to be hosted somewhere for customers to find it. Hosting and domain management is where many small businesses underinvest, and it shows the moment a visitor lands on a sluggish page.

A fast, well-built site cannot run on inadequate infrastructure. A clean design and clean code are wasted on a server that takes three seconds to respond.

Why Hosting Deserves Serious Consideration

Your website is a collection of files stored on a server connected to the internet at all times. When a visitor types your address, their browser requests those files from the server and renders them on screen.

If the server is slow, overloaded, or unavailable, the visitor gets nothing. Spinning loaders, error pages, or a blank screen. The most common outcome is that they leave for a competitor's site, and you never know they were there.

Hosting is one of the most important decisions in a website investment. It is not a place to cut corners.

The Cost of Cheap Hosting

Budget hosting plans advertised at $4.99 a month look attractive. The savings rarely outweigh the operational cost.

Low-cost providers stack hundreds, sometimes thousands, of websites onto a single server. When one of those sites spikes, every other site on the machine slows to a crawl. Page loads stretch out, visitors lose patience, and traffic bounces without an obvious cause.

Cheap hosting also typically comes with weak security, minimal customer support, and a single server in a single location that is rarely near your actual customers. The combined effect is a slow, unreliable site that undermines all the work that went into building it properly.

Why We Choose Cloudflare Pages

At UI Compass, we host every site on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare runs one of the largest content delivery networks (CDNs) on the planet — over 330 cities worldwide. Your site does not live on one server in one location. Copies of it are pushed out to every edge location the moment a build deploys, and visitors receive the version served closest to them.

If you are based in Arlington, Texas, and a customer pulls up your site from Sydney, they do not wait for data to travel across an ocean. They get it from an edge node in Sydney. Same site, same speed, anywhere in the world.

Security is built in. Cloudflare Pages is purpose-built for static sites — no database to compromise, no admin panel to break into, no plugins to exploit. DDoS protection and SSL come standard, with no extra step on your end.

Choosing the Right Domain

If you already own a domain, we transfer it to our registrar (Porkbun) and handle DNS, nameservers, and SSL certificates on your behalf. You never need to touch any of it.

If you do not have a domain yet, we help you pick the right one and register it for you. On the $150 monthly plan, the domain cost is bundled in.

A good domain is short, simple, and easy to remember. Where it works, including a service or location keyword (think "dfwroofing.com" rather than "smithco37.com") also helps with recall and search visibility.

How Should I Host My Website?

The most reliable approach is to work with someone who hosts websites professionally. Self-hosting from a home office is technically possible, but a single power outage or a flaky internet connection can take the site offline for hours at a time.

Cloudflare Pages runs across hundreds of edge locations worldwide. Even if one entire region goes down, your site stays up everywhere else.

We also keep continuous monitoring on every client site, with automatic alerts the moment something looks wrong. Most issues are addressed before a client notices.
