Our Story

Affordable, Fast Websites Built for Small Businesses

Well-designed websites that earn revenue for your business shouldn't cost a fortune. That is the principle behind why I started this company. Every business needs a website, but most small businesses simply cannot afford to spend $5,000–$10,000 up front on a professional website.

This forces small business owners toward an alternative source — engaging a freelance designer to build a site for under $1,000. The result is often little more than an online business card that fails to generate leads or revenue. It places small business owners in a frustrating position: they either cannot afford a quality website, or if they can, they do not know whom to trust to deliver one.

I set out to solve that by creating an affordable website solution for small businesses — one that is fast, search-engine optimized, and capable of generating revenue from day one. So I did just that.
