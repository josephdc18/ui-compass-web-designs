# Why Your Website Is Losing Leads

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- 53% of mobile visitors abandon a page that takes longer than 3 seconds. Already covered by 88% retention/word-of-mouth angle and bounce-rate work.
- Healthy small business conversion is 1-3%, well-optimized 3-5%. Below 1% sustained means real friction.
- The form-start vs form-completion gap: analytics that track field-level abandonment expose which question kills the submission.
- Intent mismatch: high traffic plus low conversion often means the searcher's query and the landing page do not match.
- Generic CTAs ("Submit," "Learn More," "Click Here") underperform intent-based verbs.
- Service pages with bounce rates above 60% are the diagnostic starting point, not the homepage.
