# Websites for the Trades — How to Rank on Google in My Local Area

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Customers do not search the way you describe your business. They search "toilet repairs near me," not "residential plumbing services."
- Suburbs above ~100,000 people deserve their own page. Below that, group neighboring pockets onto one page so each page has enough query volume to matter.
- Build a service-by-location matrix and ship one page per cell that has real demand. "Boiler servicing Rutland" is its own URL.
- Google Business Profile is the cheapest, fastest way to land on page one for a "near me" search. Most trades treat it as a checkbox and leave half the fields empty.
- Conversion levers that move the needle for trades: prominent phone number, tap-to-call button, a face on the site, recent photos of real jobs.
- The 2-second load target and 90+ PageSpeed score are not nice-to-haves. They are the entry fee for the local map pack.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- "2 seconds. That is how quickly your website needs to load."
- 90+ PageSpeed score target.
- ~100,000 person threshold for justifying a dedicated suburb page.
