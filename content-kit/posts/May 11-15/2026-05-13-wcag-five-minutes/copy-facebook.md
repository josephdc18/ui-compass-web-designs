Three free accessibility tests, five minutes total, that you can run on your own site this afternoon.

96% of the top million websites fail at least one of them. ADA demand-letter law firms screen for the same things.

The three tests:
1) Contrast — paste your colors into WebAIM's contrast checker
2) Keyboard — tab through your homepage and watch where focus goes
3) Screen reader — VoiceOver on Mac or Narrator on Windows, listen to the page

If any one fails, you've got a known liability — and the fix is almost always cheaper than the demand letter.

Full guide and the four most common fixes: https://uicompass.com/blog/wcag-2-2-aa-in-five-minutes/
