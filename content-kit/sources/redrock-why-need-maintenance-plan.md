# Why Your Website Needs a Maintenance Plan

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Insurance analogy: maintenance is boring, easy to put off, and you do not appreciate it until something goes catastrophically wrong.
- Silent failure modes carry real revenue: a broken contact form for a caterer = "five weeks of catering leads, gone." Most owners discover the form was broken when a customer complains months later.
- Hack recovery is not just the $3K-$5K cleanup. It is also 3 to 6 months of zero organic traffic while you rebuild rankings.
- Standing-still-is-moving-backward frame: your competitors are optimizing, Google is updating, the algorithm shifts. A static site loses ground every quarter even when nothing breaks.
- Comparative anecdote: HVAC company spent $2,800 on a hack cleanup. 14 months of preventive maintenance for the same business would have cost $2,100.
- Vendor lock-in risk inside maintenance plans: providers who register your domain in their name, or run you on a proprietary CMS only they can edit. Exit cost can dwarf the prevention cost.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- Hack-recovery anecdote: $2,800 cleanup vs $2,100 for 14 mo prevention.
- Lost-organic recovery window: 3 to 6 months.
- Half of visitors abandon past 3s (already in backlog).
- Maintenance: $50-300/mo vs breach $2K-5K+ (already in backlog).
