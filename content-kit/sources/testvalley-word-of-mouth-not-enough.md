# Why Word of Mouth Isn't Enough for a Small Local Business

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Even a referred lead Googles you before they call. Your site is the second handshake, and if it looks broken the referral dies there.
- Word-of-mouth flow is sporadic by nature. Demand looks like a sawtooth, not a line. A site smooths the gaps with inbound search.
- A bad first impression on the site cancels the referral's positive bias. Visitors form a verdict in fractions of a second.
- A local site runs 24/7. The referral conversation only happens at parties and job sites.
- One angry online review can travel further than ten happy ones. Owning the narrative on your own domain matters.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- 81% of consumers check a business's website before deciding to use them.
- 88% of users will not return after a bad website experience.
- 47% of customers will not wait longer than 2 seconds for a page to load.
- Visitors form an opinion of a site in 0.05 seconds.
