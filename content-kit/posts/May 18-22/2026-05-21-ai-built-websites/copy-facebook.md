Yes, ChatGPT can build you a website. No, it cannot keep you out of court.

AI tools are excellent at the first 60 percent of a website: layout, copy drafts, image generation, basic responsive. Use them for that.

The other 40 percent is where the trouble lives. Accessibility (ADA lawsuit risk), local SEO, real-world mobile speed, image optimization, working forms, and security patching. AI does not see these failures because the preview looks fine. The site goes live, the leads do not come, and nobody knows why.

The right workflow is hybrid. Let AI draft. Let a human ship.
