# Warning Signs Your Website is Losing Customers

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Buttons and tap targets: 48px minimum on mobile. Anything smaller is hostile to thumbs.
- Bounce rate health bands: 30-50% normal for small business, 60%+ is the warning signal that your homepage is not landing.
- Search visibility test: type your business name into Google. Then type your primary service plus your city. If you are not on page one for at least one of those, you have a visibility problem, not a "Google is hard" problem.
- Form-test cadence: submit a real test through every form on your site monthly. Silent form failures are the most expensive bug.
- Copyright footer dated 2022 or earlier signals abandoned site to both visitors and Google.
- 3-second mobile load and HTTPS padlock requirements (already covered).
