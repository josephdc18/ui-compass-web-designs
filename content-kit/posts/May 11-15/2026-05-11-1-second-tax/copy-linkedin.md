A 1-second delay costs you 7% of your conversions.

That isn't a rounding error. Multiply it by every lead your site brings in this month.

The bill is hidden because the delay never feels catastrophic. The page eventually loads. The user doesn't always bounce. But measurable conversion drops off, post after post, every second past the 2-second mark.

Here is where the second usually disappears.

1. A hero image you never optimized.
A 2.3MB photo dragged into a page builder, served at 8000px wide to a 6-inch phone. That alone is half a second before anything else loads.

2. A page-builder framework you don't see.
Most sites ship 200–400KB of jQuery, Font Awesome, and CSS resets that the page does not use. The browser pays for every byte.

3. Shared hosting.
Time to first byte on a $10/mo shared host averages 800ms. The same code on an edge network hits 60ms. Same code. Different physics.

4. Render-blocking CSS and fonts.
One Google Fonts call can hold the page hostage for 500ms before the user sees a single letter.

Each of these is cheap to fix on a hand-coded site. On a page builder it's a rebuild.
