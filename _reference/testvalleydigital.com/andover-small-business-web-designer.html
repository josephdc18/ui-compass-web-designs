Andover’s Web Development Specialists

When it comes to developing websites, look no further than Test Valley Digital. We custom-code all of our websites using HTML, CSS and JavaScript, this method of website development allows us to have full control over your website. Every line of code is written in-house, meaning we can guarantee your website will have 90+ scores on Google PageSpeed Insights.

Google PageSpeed Insights tells business owners how well their website has been developed, and ensures the website loads quickly, is accessible, has been developed using the best practices, and that it is readable by search engines. As a small service-based business in Andover, you will need to ensure that your website is getting 90+ scores on Google PageSpeed to ensure you climb the rankings on search engines, and give your customers a seamless user experience and feeling about your business. Don’t cut corners when it comes to website development, it is important that you get it right by thinking about:

  • Technologies - What technology is your website developer using to develop your website? Are they using raw code? Are they using a CMS or framework? What are the implications on your website’s performance? Are you locked into the one size fits many system?
  • Scripts and Plugins - How many scripts is your website developer going to use? Will your new website rely heavily on plugins? All of these things will weigh down your website, and will result in slower load times which will decrease the amount of leads you receive.
  • Security - Has your website been developed with your customer’s data in mind? Has your developer taken the necessary steps to protect your website from hackers? We develop static websites, meaning that there is no backend, so there is no data stored within the website to steal, it is all safely locked away in a separate location.

Does your developer have the necessary control to allow the world to be at your fingertips, or have they used a pagebuilder which won’t be able to advance much further?

SEO specialists in Andover

Contrary to popular belief, SEO (Search Engine Optimisation) isn’t a button you turn on, or something which you need to enable on your website. SEO is a strategy, something which we bring to small service-based businesses in Andover. SEO for local businesses comes in 4 forms, On-page SEO, Technical SEO, Off-Page SEO, Local SEO. When it comes to optimising your website for search engines, you need to do all 4 things well, and Google will push your website further to the top of the search results.

When executing an SEO strategy, here’s how you optimise your website:

  • On-Page SEO - Look at the keywords which people are searching for when looking for your services, for example if you’re an electrician in Andover, your main keyword will likely be Andover electrician. You then need to write effective copy which includes that keyword on multiple occasions.
  • Technical SEO - Websites need to load quickly, no objections. Google favours sites that load within 2-3 seconds,anything longer will harm your SEO and impact your Google ranking. By using custom-code, our websites are designed to be fast, mobile-friendly, secure, and built with the right technologies, ensuring they perform brilliantly for both Google and your customers.
  • Off-Page SEO - Google likes websites which are trusted by others. A crucial part of SEO is building backlinks to your website. Backlinking is getting your website linked in other locations such as: news articles, blogs, forums, information pages and other websites. When your website has been linked numerous times in other locations, particularly websites that are highly reputable, your SEO rankings will start to soar.
  • Local SEO - It’s critical to be visible to your local community, particularly as a business based in Andover. Setting up a Google Business Profile, creating localised SEO pages (just like this one) and being visible on Google Maps will help attract more local customers to your website.
