# Why Website Builders Are Failing Your Business

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Builder output is bloated by default. Every drag-drop block ships unused JS, CSS, and DOM nodes.
- The interface gets harder, not easier, the more you try to do something custom. The "easy" promise breaks the moment your site grows up.
- Integrations are gated. You can only connect to what the builder's marketplace approves, which means you cannot wire up the CRM or booking tool you actually use.
- Templates are shared. Your competitor's site looks like yours because it is yours, just with different logos.
- Builder accessibility is pass-through at best. The Domino's Pizza ADA lawsuit is the precedent every owner should know.
- Static, custom-coded sites are dramatically harder to compromise than CMS-driven ones because there is no admin login surface and no plugins to exploit.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- Names Wix, GoDaddy, and WordPress as the bloat-prone builders.
- References the Domino's Pizza accessibility lawsuit as a real legal precedent.
- "Pre-approved integrations only" is paraphrasable as a hard lock-in claim.
- Article asserts static sites are "impossible to hack" — soften to "dramatically smaller attack surface."
