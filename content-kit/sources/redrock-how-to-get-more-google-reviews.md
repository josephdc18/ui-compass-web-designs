# How to Get More Google Reviews

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- 70% of customers will leave a review if asked. Most owners simply do not ask.
- 40+ reviews unlocks ~3x clicks from Google Maps vs. fewer than 5. The threshold is non-linear.
- Timing is everything: ask right after the positive moment, not days later via email blast.
- Friction-killers: a direct Google review URL placed in the email signature, on the invoice, on a QR code at the counter, on the back of the business card, in the website footer, on the thank-you page.
- The do-not-do list: no discounts/free products/contest entries for reviews (Google penalty), no buying reviews, no review gating (screening for positive ones first), no employee or family fake reviews.
- A 24-hour follow-up SMS or email is the highest-converting channel after the in-person ask. Two or three sentences max, link included.
- "Respond to all your reviews" matters because 88% of consumers say they would use a business that does. (already covered angle, do not re-pitch.)
