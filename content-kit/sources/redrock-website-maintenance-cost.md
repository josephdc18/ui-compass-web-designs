# How Much Does Website Maintenance Cost?

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Three industry tiers: basic ($50 to $200/mo), mid ($200 to $500/mo), premium/enterprise ($500 to $5,000+/mo).
- Most providers package as Bronze/Silver/Gold. The author's critique: those tiers are designed to upsell, not to match what your site actually needs.
- "Included" usually means automated patching, backups, uptime monitoring, SSL handling. "Add-on" means hosting, domain, content writing, third-party integrations, and emergency response.
- Emergency/after-hours billing is the silent budget killer: $150 to $300/hr.
- Author's own pricing for context: $150/mo or $3,500 one-time with hosting and SSL bundled in the monthly plan.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- Basic maintenance: $50 to $200/mo.
- Mid: $200 to $500/mo.
- Enterprise: $500 to $5,000+/mo.
- Emergency/after-hours rate: $150 to $300/hr.
- Hosting line item: $10 to $100/mo.
