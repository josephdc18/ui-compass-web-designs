# Local SEO Checklist for Small Business

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- The single highest-leverage NAP failure: phone number written four different ways across the website, GBP, Yelp, and other directories. Algorithms treat that as four different businesses.
- Review velocity beats review volume. Five reviews trickling in monthly outperform thirty all in one week, because pattern naturalness is its own ranking signal.
- Hyperlocal blog content (e.g., "monsoon roof prep in Southern Utah") is competitive ground a national brand cannot occupy.
- Photo data: businesses with photos see ~42% more direction requests and ~35% more website click-throughs.
- Map-pack threshold math: less competitive markets need 20-30 reviews at 4.5+; competitive markets need 100+. The benchmark is your competitor count, not a fixed number.
- Direct review URLs (the place_id-based Google link) cut friction vs. asking customers to "find us and click stars."

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- Photos: 42% more direction requests, 35% more website clicks.
- 60%+ of local searches happen on mobile.
- Map pack threshold: 20-30 reviews (low-comp), 100+ (high-comp).
- Realistic local SEO timeline to measurable results: 3-6 months.
