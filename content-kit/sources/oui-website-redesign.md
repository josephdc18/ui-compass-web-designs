# Website Redesign Guide

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Seven concrete warning signs: poor mobile, slow load, outdated look, low conversion, declining traffic over 6+ months, technical breakage, customer complaints.
- Redesign-vs-update threshold: 0-1 signs = optimize, 2-3 signs = targeted redesign, 4+ signs = full rebuild.
- Cadence rule: major redesign every 3-5 years, minor refresh every 6-12 months.
- Five-stage process: discovery, content + IA, design, dev + test, launch + 30-day monitor.
- Traditional agency timeline: 3-6 months. Streamlined studios: 2-3 weeks. Hand-coded with content ready: even tighter.
- Hidden costs nobody quotes: content creation $500-$3K, photography $200-$1K, monthly maintenance $50-$300.
