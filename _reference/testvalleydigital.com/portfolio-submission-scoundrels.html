Petherwick Jiu-Jitsu

One of our longest-serving clients, Petherwick Jiu-Jitsu have reaped the most from our monthly web packages, from continuous, unlimited support and edits, to being the first of our clients to benefit from a free website redesign

Starting as a modest grappling club renting space from their competitors, Petherwick Jiu-Jitsu has since evolved into a fully-fledged martial arts gym with their own location in Tidworth. A significant part of this transformation can be attributed to Test Valley Digital's efforts. We helped Chris Petherwick, the owner, turn an overlooked Wix website into a powerful online presence that, at it's best, attracts five outreach inquiries per month.

How We Helped Petherwick Jiu-JitsuOver the Years

  • Full Website Design and Development

    Striking a balance between the welcoming nature of the club, while maintaining the "underground" aspect of grappling sport, proved to be a challenge. Nevertheless, through the use of intersection graphics and a red colour palette, we feel a good middle-ground was found.

  • Keyword-Driven Copy

    With little competition in the Tidworth area, a wider keyword strategy was adopted. From "Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu" to "Martial Arts" and "Gym", Petherwick Jiu-Jitsu are able to find success to a large number of search queries, certainly contributing to their success.

  • Full Redesign

    The club relocated from Andover to Tidworth right around the time the club was due for a website redesign. We jumped on this opportunity to help the club pivot to their new location, dominate the competition, and put more emphasis on the new amenities offered at the gym.

Boosted Membership Rates and Justified Costs

Over the past two years, Petherwick Jiu-Jitsu have seen tremendous value from our partnership. Moving away from Wix has significantly benefited their business, and by entrusting their web duties to Test Valley Digital, Chris has been able to spend more time on the mat with his students.

During one of their best weeks to date, Petherwick Jiu-Jitsu received five email inquiries through the website, all of which converted to paying club members. With a membership fee of £60 per month, this resulted in an immediate return on their website investment, demonstrating that a Test Valley Digital website is an obvious choice for businesses with a strong offer to promote.
