# Web Design and Development Guide

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Six phases: discovery, design + wireframe, development, QA, launch, maintenance.
- Timeline: simple 2-4 weeks, custom 4-8 weeks, complex 3-6 months.
- Common failures: skipped discovery, desktop-only design, outdated stack, broken backups at launch.
- Maintenance cadence: weekly backups, monthly content audits, ongoing A/B testing.
