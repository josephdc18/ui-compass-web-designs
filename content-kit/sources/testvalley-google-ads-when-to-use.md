# When Should Small Businesses Use Google Ads?

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Google Ads is not table stakes. For a low-competition local service, a fast site plus proper on-page SEO does the job for free.
- Quick rule of thumb: open the search you want to rank for. If fewer than 5 sponsored ads sit at the top, organic alone will likely win it.
- Three legit cases for ad spend: ecommerce (technical search competitiveness), saturated industries where SEO is a knife fight, and high-ticket B2B/luxury where one customer pays for a year of clicks.
- Running ads to a slow site is setting money on fire. Quality Score punishes load time, so CPC climbs.
- Keyword Planner is the cheap tool to size the fight before paying for it.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- "47% of users won't wait longer than 2 seconds" for a page to load.
- The 5-sponsored-competitor cutoff for "ads vs no ads."
- Meaningful ad spend is in "the £1000s per month" range.
