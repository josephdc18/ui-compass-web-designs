# Website Maintenance Checklist

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Maintenance work splits cleanly by cadence: weekly (uptime, security scans, backup verification, form tests), monthly (updates, broken links, speed, analytics, mobile), quarterly (full security audit, SEO review, browser/device testing, DB optimization, restore-from-backup test), annual (SSL renewal, domain renewal, hosting evaluation, design refresh, content audit, legal compliance).
- Most owners skip everything because months 1 to 3 feel fine. That is exactly when the neglect compounds.
- Neglect timeline: months 1 to 3 invisible, 3 to 6 speed and security drift, 6 to 12 ranking drops and stale content, year 1+ hacks, blacklisting, $3K to $5K to recover.
- Form tests and broken-link checks are skipped most often because they have no visible urgency.
- Tooling the author actually uses (no affiliate spin): UptimeRobot, PageSpeed Insights, Search Console, Screaming Frog, GTmetrix, GA4.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- Hack/blacklist recovery cost: $3,000 to $5,000.
- Neglect-to-disaster window: roughly 12 months.
- Owner-cited toolset: UptimeRobot, Screaming Frog, GTmetrix, PSI, Search Console, GA4.
