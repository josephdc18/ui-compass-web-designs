Our Process

When working with an agency, your website shouldn’t be a headache. Leave it to the experts to do all the hard work while you run your business. Our process is simple for that very reason, with most websites delivered within 4-6 weeks.

  • Getting Started

    Have a chat with one of our team members who will learn what you’re trying to achieve. We can work out the number of pages you’ll need and a sitemap that works. We set up a contract and send over a questionnaire for you to answer. You can then be as involved, or not, as you want.

    01

  • Design

    We will design you a bespoke website based on everything we’ve learnt about you, helping you connect with your customers. We share a design in Figma, a well known design tool, where you can make comments and request changes.

    02

  • Content

    We write your website’s copy and choose the right images to stand out. We can use stock images, or you can share any pictures you have with us to use on the website. We can also work with professional copywriters at an extra cost, or leave it to us, and our in-house copywriting tool can write copy for you.

    03

  • Development

    When we have the look and feel of your website sorted, we develop everything using custom code. We can share a link to a demo website so you can see how it is coming along, and you can also request any changes you’d like along the way.

    04

  • QA and Go Live

    We then go through thorough quality assurance checks, ensuring your website is responsive, accessible, fast and something you’ll be proud of. We then begin the domain transfer process, and then put your new website live for you.

    05

  • Maintenance

    Leave the rest to us! We manage all of the changes and updates to your website, so your website will be perfect 24/7.
