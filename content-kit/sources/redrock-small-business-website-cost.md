# Small Business Website Cost Breakdown

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Costs sort into five tiers: DIY platforms ($0 to $500 upfront plus $15 to $50/mo), freelancer ($1,500 to $5,000), small agency ($3,000 to $10,000), mid agency ($10K to $25K+), enterprise ($25K to $100K+).
- Most small businesses land between $2,000 and $7,000 for a professional build.
- Hidden line items the owner forgets to budget for: copywriting ($500 to $2,000), stock photography or shoots, SEO setup beyond the basics, and hourly edits at $50 to $150/hr after launch.
- DIY locks you into a perpetual rental. A 3-year Squarespace bill ($1,188) already exceeds what some custom builds cost outright, and you do not own the result.
- Freelancers cost less than agencies but vary wildly in reliability and communication. Agencies bake in process and rarely ghost.
