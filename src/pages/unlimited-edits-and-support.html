Unlimited Edits and Support

Our Monthly Subscription package includes unlimited website edits and ongoing support. Every change request is governed by a defined service-level agreement, ensuring that updates are completed within a clearly stated timeframe.

One of the most common frustrations with website agencies is the long delay between requesting a change and seeing it go live. Websites become outdated quickly, and when changes are eventually made, the cost is often unpredictable. Our model is designed to remove that friction.

Our Monthly Website Package

The Monthly Subscription is built around a single principle: a website should be a living asset, not a static project that ages out of relevance. Running a small business is demanding, and you should not have to manage the technical or content updates yourself.

When something needs to change, you contact us directly. We make the change — at no additional cost.

Our Packages

What "Unlimited" Means

Unlimited means exactly that: you can request updates and revisions to any of your pages as often as you wish, with no extra charges. Every change is included in the monthly fee.

When a change is requested, we will also share our perspective on the impact. If a proposed change could affect performance or SEO, we will let you know before proceeding. Ultimately the decision is yours, and we will deliver what you ask for.

Typical requests include adding or revising text, replacing images, introducing new sections, and refreshing existing layouts. The two changes outside the scope of unlimited edits are the addition of entirely new pages and converting a page into a different page type (for example, replacing an "About" page with a "Services" page), as the latter would have measurable SEO consequences.

A representative example is one of our clients, who wanted a booking system on her website but had not yet selected a provider. Once she made her choice, we integrated it into her site at no additional cost. The result is a website that grows with her business, managed end-to-end by our team.

Understanding the Value

Unlimited edits are exclusive to our Monthly Subscription, and the financial case is most apparent when measured over a three-year horizon. Most agencies bill in one-hour minimums for hourly work, which is intended to encourage clients to batch requests and to cover communication and project-management overhead. The result is that a two-minute text update can cost the equivalent of an hour — typically $80 to $150.
