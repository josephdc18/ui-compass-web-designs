What Do we Mean by "Unlimited"

By unlimited, we mean that you can have any of your pages updated and changed as often as you'd like, with no additional charges. Everything is included in the monthly website package.

When requesting changes, we can also advise on what the best change is and let you know if the change could be detrimental to your website. It is your website though, so if you want a change, a change you will get!

For most clients, this will often be adding text, changing images, making a new section here or there, and giving things a little refresh from time to time. The only limitation with these are the addition of new pages, or completely changing your "About" page to a "Services" page, for example. The latter is pretty bad for your SEO, anyway!=

A great example is Jazmine, from Essence of Motherhood. She wanted a booking system on her website, but didn't know which one to pick. She decided to wait until later, and then picked one. As part of our unlimited edits, we integrated it into the website for her, at no extra cost. We have a happy client, and she gets her website managed by the experts. A win, win!
