# How Many Pages Should a Small Business Website Have?

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Core five: home, about, services, contact, blog/resources. Add service-area or per-service pages from there.
- 7-page sample structure for a local electrician (covered already in backlog).
- Compounding asset: one quality blog post per month = 12 new indexed pages a year, 24 in two years. Each targets a different query.
- Keyword cannibalization warning: two pages targeting the same query split your ranking power and confuse Google about which to surface.
- Location pages with city-swapped boilerplate get filtered. Genuine local differentiation (real photos, real testimonials, local landmark references) is the only version that ranks.
- Placeholder/coming-soon pages are credibility leaks. Better to ship five strong pages than seven with two stubs.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- One blog post a month = 24 indexed pages in two years.
- Homepage 5-second comprehension test: "what you do, who you serve, where you are, all visible in 5 seconds."
- Single-services-page rule of thumb: combine if 2-3 offerings, split if 6+.
