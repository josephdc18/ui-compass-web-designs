# How Long Does It Take to Build a Website?

## Key themes (paraphrased)
- Industry benchmarks by site type: landing page 3-5 days, small business 5-7 page site 2-4 weeks, larger 10-20 page site 4-8 weeks, e-commerce/web app 8-16 weeks.
- A small business build is roughly 40-60 hours of actual development. The rest of the calendar is wait time on the client side.
- ~40% of total project time is wait. Slow client feedback is the single biggest schedule killer.
- The four-week structure: discovery, design, build, test/launch.
- Content-ready-at-kickoff (copy, photos, logo, brand assets) is the single biggest accelerator. Without it, week 1 stretches into week 3.
- The trade-off framing: page builders ship faster initially but cost more to maintain. Custom takes longer up front, less over the lifetime.

## Quotable claims / stats (verify before reuse)
- 40-60 hours of dev work for a small business site.
- 40% of project calendar time is wait time on client feedback.
- Industry small business build: 2-4 weeks.
- Feedback turn benchmark: 2-3 days for fast projects.
