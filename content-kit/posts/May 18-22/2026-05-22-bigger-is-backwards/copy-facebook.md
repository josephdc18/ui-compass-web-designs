"I'll get a website when I'm bigger" is the most common reason small business owners delay launching one.

The sentence has cause and effect reversed. The website is what gets you bigger. Waiting until you are "ready" is waiting for the result to fund the cause.

Every month you do not have a site, three things happen. Customers search and find your competitor. Brand interest from your other marketing dead-ends. Google ranking equity does not accumulate. None of that comes back.

The cleanest move is the inverse. Build small, build early. Let it accumulate while the business grows. By the time you are "big enough to deserve" a real site, the site has already been the engine.
