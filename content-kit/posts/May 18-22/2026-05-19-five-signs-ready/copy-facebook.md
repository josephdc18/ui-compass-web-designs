Five signs you are ready for a website. Two signs you are not.

The five that mean go: customers ask for a URL, you're paying for traffic that has nowhere to land, your competitor has a site and you don't, you can describe your service in one sentence, and you have at least one piece of real credibility (a review, a credential, a customer photo).

The two that mean wait: your offer is still fuzzy (fix that first, the site cannot save a vague offer), or you have zero reviews and zero portfolio yet (spend ninety days getting one).

Most small business owners we work with are at three or four out of five. They were always ready. The guilt was the only thing in the way.
