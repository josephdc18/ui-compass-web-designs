Agile Claim App

Not every website needs the full SEO treatment, content-heavy pages, or lead-generation functionality. Some websites, particularly B2B ones, simply need to look professional and effectively direct traffic to their ideal customer profile.

That's exactly what we delivered for Agile Claim's flagship app. As a white-labelled solution, potential enterprise clients require a rigorous scoping process regardless. We helped Agile Claim transition from a slow-loading, basic WordPress site to something that communicates "trust" and "security" - fundamental principles in the insurance industry.

Visit the Website

Reducing Load Times By Over 75%

  • Green PageSpeed Guarantee

    Clients transitioning from page builders typically see the most dramatic impact from our performance guarantees. We reduced Agile Claim's website load time from 10.0s to below 2.5s (on a Slow 4G connection).

  • Smart Use of Assets

    To showcase their product effectively, Agile Claim requested a rotating carousel of partner logos and a feature video. Through optimised libraries and lazy loading techniques, we implemented these features whilst maintaining our Green PageSpeed Guarantee.

  • Modern, SaaS Design

    First impressions are everything. As a company operating in the automotive insurance sector, we delivered a design that's not only crisp and contemporary, but also instils trust and confidence in users.

A Partnership Was Born

Our efforts resulted in a clean, fast-loading site that enabled Agile Claim to put their best foot forward, effectively showcasing their extensive partnerships within the insurance industry.

However, with a portfolio of other insurance-focused businesses under their umbrella, our collaboration extended far beyond this single project.

Moving forward, Test Valley Digital was entrusted with Enterprise Insurance - an emerging insurance solutions provider for tradespeople. Agile can now operate with confidence, knowing they have a trusted, single point of contact for all their websites, maintained and supported for years to come.
