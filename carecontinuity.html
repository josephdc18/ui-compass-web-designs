Built for every stakeholder in the Care Journey

​

For Patients

Every day hundreds of patients go home with discharge instructions and referral guidance. Care Continuity offers concierge navigation services to help these patients match with in-network providers and close the loop on follow-up care.
​

For Health Systems

Health systems are focused on keeping valuable patients aligned with the health system while improving quality outcomes and access. Care Continuity's smart navigation programs improve network integrity and drive service line growth.
​

For Providers

Specialist providers and PCPs have limited capacity available to expand access and reduce appointment wait times. Care Continuity helps stratify high-impact referrals to ensure the right patients are prioritized and provider schedules are aligned with outcomes.
