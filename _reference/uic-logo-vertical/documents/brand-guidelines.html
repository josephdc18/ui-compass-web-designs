Brand Guidelines

A quick reference for using your brand assets correctly.

Logo Usage

Your logo is the primary identifier of your brand. Use it consistently across all materials.

On Light Backgrounds

Use logo-transparent.png

On Dark Backgrounds

Clear Space

Always maintain clear space around the logo equal to at least 25% of the logo height.

Minimum clear space = Height of logo ÷ 4

Minimum Size

To ensure legibility, don't use the logo smaller than these sizes:

  • Print: 25mm / 1 inch width
  • Digital: 80px width

What Not To Do

  • Don't stretch or distort the logo
  • Don't rotate the logo
  • Don't change the logo colors
  • Don't add effects like shadows or gradients
  • Don't place the logo on busy backgrounds
  • Don't recreate or redraw the logo
